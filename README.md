# Civspeak

**A voice Civilopedia for Civilization VI.**

Ask the Civilopedia out loud while you play. What does a Ziggurat do? Where can I build a Mbanza?
How do I get Great Prophets? George reads out a short answer, and the full Civilopedia entries
appear on screen.

Civspeak reads the Civilopedia from **your own game**: your expansions, your DLC and your mods.

## Download

For Windows 10 and Windows 11.

1. Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/OllySalanson/civspeak/releases/latest).
2. Under **Assets**, click **Civspeak-Setup.exe** to download it.
3. Open the downloaded file to install Civspeak.

### If Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

Civspeak is new, so Windows does not know it yet. You may see a blue box.

1. Click **More info**.
2. Click **Run anyway**.

You only need to do this once.

## The first time

- Civspeak finds Civilization VI by itself. If it cannot, it asks you to choose the game's folder.
- It downloads George's voice (268 MB). This happens once.
- **Load a game in Civ VI once.** Civspeak reads the rules of the last game you loaded.

## Ask a question

1. In Civ VI, **hold the `` ` `` key** (the key under Esc).
2. Ask your question.
3. Let go.

George answers out loud. You stay in the game.

- **Tap** `` ` `` instead of holding it, and Civspeak listens until you stop talking.
- **Esc** stops George.
- You can also **type** a question in the Civspeak window.

Civspeak is free, with nothing to sign up for.

## The star by the clock

Civspeak sits by the clock as a gold star. Click it to:

- open the Civspeak window,
- start Civspeak when you log in to Windows,
- turn Civspeak off.

Closing the window does not turn Civspeak off. `` ` `` still works in the game.

## Updates

Civspeak checks for a new version once a day. When there is one, the star tells you. Your settings
and George's voice are kept.

## Privacy

- Your voice is turned into text **on your PC**. No audio leaves it.
- Your question and the matching Civilopedia entries are sent to an online service, which writes the
  short answer.

## About

Civspeak is an unofficial fan tool. It is not made, endorsed or supported by Firaxis Games or 2K.
It contains no game files or text: it reads them from the copy of Civilization VI on your PC.
