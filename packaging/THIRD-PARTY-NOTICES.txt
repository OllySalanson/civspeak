Civspeak - third-party software and models
==========================================

Civspeak is an unofficial fan tool. It is not made, endorsed or supported by Firaxis Games or 2K.
It ships no game text, art or files: it reads the Civilopedia from the copy of Civilization VI
installed on your own PC. Your questions and the matching Civilopedia entries are sent to an online
service, which writes the short answer.

Civspeak's own source code: https://github.com/OllySalanson/civspeak

The installed program contains the software below, each under its own licence. The full licence
texts are at the addresses given, and the exact source of each version used is on PyPI
(https://pypi.org/project/<name>/<version>/) or at the project's own address.

Models downloaded on first run
------------------------------
- Kokoro-82M voice model and voices (Apache-2.0), https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M,
  as converted to ONNX by kokoro-onnx: https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx/releases

  https://huggingface.co/Systran/faster-whisper-base.en
They are fetched from Civspeak's own GitHub Release and kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Civspeak\models.
